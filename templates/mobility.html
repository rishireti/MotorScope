Pull your data from the Health app

MotorScope reads your measurements from an Apple Health export rather than asking you to type them in. Export everything from Health on your iPhone and upload the ZIP here. MotorScope reads only the mobility measurements and files them as readings you can compare over time.

How to export your data

  1. Open the Health app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the top-right corner.
  3. Scroll down and select Export All Health Data.
  4. Tap Export and choose a location or app to save the ZIP file.

Health takes a while to build the export and may ask you to wait while it prepares. You will end up with a file called apple_health_export.zip. Choose that file below, not any file inside it.

Only mobility samples are read. Everything else in the export is skipped, and the ZIP is deleted as soon as it has been read.

A Health export contains your complete medical history, so treat the ZIP as a private file. MotorScope reads it on this computer only, keeps nothing but the readings you save, and never sends it anywhere.

Sources

Where the expected ranges come from

The expected ranges above are MotorScope's own conservative starting points, chosen with reference to the following public sources. They are not clinical thresholds, and they are not compared against other people or any published outcome.

  1. Measuring Walking Quality Through iPhone Mobility Metrics (Apple) — walking speed, step length, double support time, and walking asymmetry.
  2. Six-minute walk distance in healthy subjects: reference standards from a general population sample — six-minute walk distance.
  3. Track your cardio fitness levels (Apple Support) — cardio fitness (VO2 max).
  4. Reference values for running and walking gait metrics — ground contact time, vertical oscillation, and running stride length.
  5. Stair performance in healthy adults — stair ascent and descent speed.

MotorScope is a tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, rule out, or monitor any condition. Discuss any changes you notice with a qualified clinician.
