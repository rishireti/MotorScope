{{ section_header('', 'How to use these alongside MotorScope') }}

These sources are a good place to understand mobility in general and to find practical ways to stay active. MotorScope adds one specific thing: a record of your movement over time, so you can describe what has changed for you rather than only what is typical for people in general.

If something in your movement concerns you, please discuss it with a healthcare professional. Reading is a useful complement to that conversation, not a substitute for it.
